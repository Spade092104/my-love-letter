For My Love

My dearest love,

To the girl who holds my heart

❤

Yesterday, when I heard that you were in the hospital because of a headache, my heart felt heavy and my mind could not stop worrying. I wanted to be there with you, to hold your hand and tell you that everything was going to be alright. But I cannot be there because of the distance between us, and I hope you know that even from afar, my heart is always with you.

Seeing you hurt, even for a moment, reminded me how deeply I love you. You are not just someone I care about—you are the peace in my chaos, the smile in my hardest days, and the dream I never want to wake up from. Every time I think of you, my heart becomes softer, warmer, and more grateful.

I hope you are resting, healing, and feeling better every single day. I am so proud of your strength, and I am so lucky to love someone as beautiful, kind, and precious as you. Please take care of yourself, my love. I am always with you, even from afar.

My love for you is not loud, but it is steady.

And it will always be yours.

With all my love,

Your forever person
